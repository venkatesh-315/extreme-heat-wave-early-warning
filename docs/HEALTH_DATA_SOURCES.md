# Official Indian Epidemiological & Heat Health Data Sources Catalog

**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Last Updated:** August 31, 2026  
**Scope:** Evaluation of Official Government of India & State Government Health Datasets for Heatwave Early Warning & Risk Modeling.

---

## 1. Important Regulatory & Methodological Disclosure

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **No real-time, daily India-wide mortality or hospitalization API exists.**  
> In accordance with Indian statistical publication cycles and epidemiological standards:
> 1. All mortality figures generated by the ThermoGuard real-time inference engine are **model-estimated risks** ($0-100\%$), not active clinical death counts.
> 2. General all-cause mortality records are **never equated** to heat-attributable mortality unless defined by explicit forensic/clinical heatstroke diagnostic criteria.
> 3. General hospital admissions are **never equated** to heat-related admissions without explicit heat illness / syndromic ICD-10 attribution.

## 2. Catalog of Official Indian Health Data Sources

### Dataset 1: Integrated Disease Surveillance Programme (IDSP) — Heat-Related Illness Surveillance
- **Source Agency:** National Centre for Disease Control (NCDC), Ministry of Health & Family Welfare (MoHFW), Government of India
- **Dataset Name:** National Heat-Related Illness & Death Weekly Surveillance Summary
- **Portal:** [ncdc.mohfw.gov.in](https://ncdc.mohfw.gov.in) / [idsp.mohfw.gov.in](https://idsp.mohfw.gov.in)
- **Geographic Granularity:** State & Participating District Hospitals
- **Temporal Granularity:** Weekly / Cumulative Seasonal (March – July)
- **Target Definition:** Confirmed Heatstroke Cases, Suspected Heatstroke Cases, Reported Heatstroke Deaths
- **Suitability for ML Training:** **SUITABLE_FOR_CALIBRATION (Conditional)**
  - *Rationale:* Provides official clinical counts of severe heatstroke morbidity and mortality during active heat seasons. Suitable for calibrating threshold alert boundaries and regional risk weightings.
- **Key Limitations:** Weekly aggregation limits single-day lag feature attribution; rural non-reporting bias exists in remote PHCs.

---

### Dataset 2: Sample Registration System (SRS) — Medical Certification of Cause of Death (MCCD)
- **Source Agency:** Office of the Registrar General & Census Commissioner, Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India
- **Dataset Name:** Report on Medical Certification of Cause of Death (MCCD)
- **Portal:** [censusindia.gov.in](https://censusindia.gov.in) / [data.gov.in](https://data.gov.in)
- **Geographic Granularity:** State / Major Urban Agglomerations
- **Temporal Granularity:** Annual
- **Target Definition:** All-Cause Mortality classified under ICD-10 chapters (including Chapter XX: External causes, Exposure to excessive natural heat `X30`)
- **Suitability for ML Training:** **NOT_SUITABLE_FOR_TARGET**
  - *Rationale:* Annual reporting delay (12–24 months) and lack of daily temporal granularity prevents direct training of daily heatwave prediction regressors without mathematical excess-mortality modeling.
- **Key Limitations:** Medically certified coverage is primarily urban (~35% of total registered deaths nationwide).

---

### Dataset 3: National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) & State Heat Action Plan Reports
- **Source Agency:** NDMA in coordination with State Disaster Management Authorities (SDMAs) of Telangana, Maharashtra, Gujarat, Odisha, and Rajasthan
- **Dataset Name:** State Heat Action Plan Annual Review & Heatwave Casualty Reports
- **Portal:** [ndma.gov.in](https://ndma.gov.in) / State Health & Relief Commissioner Portals
- **Geographic Granularity:** District / Municipal Corporation Level (e.g., Ahmedabad AMC, Hyderabad GHMC, Bhubaneswar BMC)
- **Temporal Granularity:** Daily / Episode-based during active Red/Orange alerts
- **Target Definition:** Verified Heatwave-Attributable Casualties (ex-gratia verified death audits)
- **Suitability for ML Training:** **SUITABLE_FOR_CALIBRATION**
  - *Rationale:* Highly scrutinized forensic audits following state-specific Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) verifying meteorological causality.
- **Key Limitations:** Limited to states with active municipal Heat Action Plans (HAPs).

---

### Dataset 4: National Health Mission (NHM) — Health Management Information System (HMIS)
- **Source Agency:** Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW), Government of India
- **Dataset Name:** Monthly District Facility Inpatient & Outpatient Utilization Summary
- **Portal:** [data.gov.in](https://data.gov.in) / [hmis.mohfw.gov.in](https://hmis.mohfw.gov.in)
- **Geographic Granularity:** District / Primary Health Centre (PHC)
- **Temporal Granularity:** Monthly
- **Target Definition:** Total Inpatient Admissions, Emergency Case Attendances
- **Suitability for ML Training:** **NOT_SUITABLE_FOR_TARGET**
  - *Rationale:* High-level hospital admission numbers contain all diagnostic categories (maternal, surgical, infectious) without specific heat attribution.

## 3. Provenance & Operational Rules Summary

| Dataset Status Code | Meaning in ThermoGuard | UI Display Treatment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `UNAVAILABLE` | No verified official real-time clinical health feed is available for this location today. | Displays `"Health Data: UNAVAILABLE"` badge. Displays model-estimated risk only. |
| `HISTORICAL` | Official retrospective government health data exists for research and calibration. | Displays `"Historical observed data"` in dedicated historical analytics views. |
| `VALIDATED_TRAINING_DATA` | Verified retrospective heat-attributable health data was used in offline model calibration. | Displays `"Model: Calibrated with Verified Historical Data"`. |
